A spreadsheet will tell you a designer is at 78% utilization and call that healthy. It won't tell you that 78% is spread across six context switches a day, or that the same person absorbed every fire drill this month because they're the one who never says no.

Running resourcing across a blended team of designers and engineers on ten concurrent clients taught me that the number that predicts burnout isn't utilization — it's fragmentation. Someone booked at 60% across two focused projects is in better shape than someone booked at 60% across five.

So I stopped optimizing the percentage and started protecting the shape of people's weeks: fewer, longer blocks per person, and a real conversation before adding a sixth thing to anyone's plate, no matter what the tracker said was available.
