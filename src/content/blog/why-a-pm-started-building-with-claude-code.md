I'd spent years translating between clients and engineers without ever having built anything myself. I could write a clean acceptance criterion and still not have a real feel for why "just add a filter" sometimes takes an afternoon and sometimes takes a sprint.

Building a small app end to end with Claude Code closed that gap faster than any amount of shadowing ever did. Watching a "simple" change ripple through data model, API, and UI in real time recalibrated how I scope work and how skeptical I am of the word "quick" in a planning meeting.

It also changed how I show up with engineering teams. I'm not pretending to be a developer. But I can read a diff, ask a sharper question in a design review, and tell the difference between a real blocker and a preference — and that credibility is worth more to a delivery relationship than any process I could bring to it.
